Combat Sequence

The typical hand to hand combat sequence goes like this:

Step One: Everybody rolls for initiative. The character with the highest initiative roll strikes first. the ones next in line follow in de­ scending order.

Step Two: The one with initiative rolls to strike a particular oppo­ nent and adds in any strike bonuses he may have.

Step Three: The character under attack can attempt to roll a parry or dodge. The high roll wins. Defender always wins tics.

After a battle, characters who have sustained physieal damage must tend to their wounds.
